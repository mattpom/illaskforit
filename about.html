About This Site

If you want to know what this is, who runs it, and why it exists — you're in the right place. It won't take long.

What Is I'll Ask For It?

It's an opinion site. That's it. There are no news articles here, no sponsored posts, no affiliate links disguised as recommendations, and no attempt to be objective. Every piece on this site is someone's opinion — specifically, mine.

The name comes from the phrase everyone already knows: "If I want your opinion, I'll ask for it." The joke is that this whole site is the asking. You showed up. You get the opinion.

"These aren't news. They're not guides. They're not professional reviews. They're what one guy thinks about bagels, Steve Perry, Buffalo Louie's, and San Sebastián. That's the whole thing."

The Rules

There are no recipes. If you want a recipe, the internet has approximately four billion of them.

Everything is subjective. I'm not the final authority on anything here. I'm just the guy who had the time to write it down.

You're allowed to disagree. That's actually the point. An opinion nobody argues with isn't doing its job.

No sponsored content. Nothing here was paid for by anyone. I bought everything I ate, visited every place I wrote about, and formed every opinion without a check attached to it.

The Tagline

Nothing But Time and Opinions. That's what this site has. Time enough to write it. Opinions strong enough to put them somewhere permanent. If that sounds like something worth reading, you're already in the right place.
