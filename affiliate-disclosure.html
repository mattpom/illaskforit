The short version: some links on this site make us a few cents if you buy something. Here's the whole story.

Some of the links on I'll Ask For It are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a small commission. This costs you nothing extra — the price is the same whether you use our link or type in the address yourself.

3. How This Affects Our Opinions

It doesn't. Every opinion on this Site was formed before any affiliate link was ever attached to it. We link to things we'd recommend anyway, add a tracking tag to the link, and if you happen to buy one, we get a few cents. Nobody pays us to say something is good. Nobody sends us free stuff in exchange for coverage. If we ever change that policy, we'll update this page.

4. Which Pages Have Affiliate Links

Not every page on this Site contains affiliate links. Where they do appear, they're typically in list-style or gift-guide content — the kind of page where "here's a thing you could buy" is naturally part of the format. Opinion pieces, articles, and rankings without a natural product tie-in generally don't include them.
