Go for the Wings, Not the Carrots

BuffaLouie's has been an Indiana tradition since 1987. Now Carmel gets its own taste of the legend. The carrots will come with your order. Ignore them.

I'll Ask For It · Food & Drink · Carmel, IN

If you've ever been to Bloomington, there's a good chance someone pointed you toward The Gables. That distinctive building on Indiana University's doorstep has been home to BuffaLouie's for years — and it's the kind of place that inspires the sort of loyalty that follows people across the state. Now, Carmel residents no longer need to make the drive south, because BuffaLouie's has officially spread its wings to the Indianapolis suburbs, setting up shop at 43 W. Main Street in the heart of Carmel's Arts & Design District.

The story starts in 1987, when Jay Lieser — a Buffalo, New York native and Indiana University student — decided to bring the Buffalo chicken wing to Bloomington during his junior year. He named the restaurant after his grandfather, Louie Lieser, and the iconic mustachioed logo on everything from the sign to the merch? That's a caricature of Grandpa Lou himself, cigar and all. Using original family recipes and homemade sauces, the restaurant grew from a spot on 17th Street into a Bloomington institution at The Gables Building, and eventually earned a spot on USA Today's list of the top 10 wing joints in the country. That's not a local brag. That's a national verdict.

The Carmel location carries that legacy forward with serious Hoosier credibility behind it. Co-owner Nick Williams is an IU grad and former BuffaLouie's employee who clearly never forgot where the best wings in the state came from. Joining him as a partner is Yogi Ferrell — Indiana University basketball standout, NBA veteran, and a name that carries real weight in this state. Walk through the door and you'll immediately understand why that partnership makes sense. The walls are lined with Indiana University sports memorabilia and decor that gives the space the feeling of an extension of Assembly Hall's spirit. On game days, the atmosphere alone is worth showing up for before you even order a single wing.

"From The Gables in Bloomington to Main Street in Carmel — some institutions earn the right to expand."

Now, about those wings. The menu is admirably focused. You can order traditional bone-in or boneless, in sizes ranging from a Single (10 wings) all the way up to a Super Bucket of 100. The sauce options are where things get interesting — and extensive. The Buffalo lineup runs from Mild through Medium, Hot, TNT, Volcanic, and all the way up to the ominously named Loucifer. The BBQ column is anchored by Mrs. Louie's Famous Sweet Q, with Q variations across every heat level. The Exotic category covers everything from Teriyaki and Honey Garlic to Korean Q, Rasta Jerk, and Asian Zing. Dry rub fans get Montreal Steak and Memphis Q. There is a sauce for everyone, and there is no excuse to order timidly.

The rest of the menu is built to complement the wings rather than compete with them. Chicken sandwiches come grilled, crispy, or spicy crispy, with standouts like the Buffalo Style (provolone and medium buffalo sauce) and The Pacer Parm (marinara and provolone). Burgers are two all-beef quarter-pound smash patties — the lineup includes the All-American, the Sweet Lou smothered in Mrs. Louie's Famous Sweet Q, The Main St. with jalapeno and avocado, and the HeisMendoza. Sides cover tater tots, fries, onion rings with Louie's 87 Sauce, loaded fries or tots, and cheese bread. Salads are available for anyone who genuinely wants one.

And that brings us to the carrots. They will arrive with your wings. They are fine. They are there. But BuffaLouie's is not a carrot destination. It is a wing destination that happens to include carrots as a courtesy gesture toward the concept of vegetables. Order accordingly.

↑ The Verdict

ORDER THE WINGS. SKIP THE CARROTS. THANK YOURSELF LATER.

BuffaLouie's Carmel is the rare expansion that feels earned. It's rooted in a genuine Indiana tradition — born at The Gables in Bloomington in 1987, built on family recipes and homemade sauces, and now brought north by people who actually care about getting it right. The IU sports decor, the Yogi Ferrell partnership, the sauce wall that requires a decision — all of it adds up to a place with real identity in a suburb that doesn't always have enough of them. The wings are the reason to go. Everything else is a pleasant bonus. The carrots are decorative.

BuffaLouie's Carmel — 43 W. Main Street, Carmel, IN 46032 · 317-533-0488 · Sun–Wed 11am–9pm, Thu–Sat 11am–10pm
