The Whole Point

"If I want your opinion, I'll ask for it."

The implied joke is that you never actually ask. You just start talking. Which is what this site is. No one requested these opinions. No editor assigned them. No algorithm promoted them. They exist because they needed to exist, and because someone had the time to write them down and a website to put them on.
