Privacy Policy

This site collects almost nothing. Here's exactly what that means.

Effective Date: June 10, 2026  ·  Last Updated: July 15, 2026

I'll Ask For It ("the Site," "we," "us," or "our") is a personal opinion website operated by an individual at illaskforit.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information — if any — is collected when you visit the Site, how it is used, and what rights you have regarding that information.

By accessing or using the Site, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

No personally identifiable information is collected directly by this Site. There are no account registrations, contact forms, newsletter signups, or comment systems. You can read every page on this Site without providing any information about yourself.

However, certain information may be collected automatically through third-party services used to deliver this Site:

GitHub Pages (Hosting): This Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. GitHub may collect server log data including your IP address, browser type, referring/exit pages, and timestamps when you access the Site. This data is subject to GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Google Fonts: This Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, a service provided by Google LLC. When fonts are loaded, your browser makes a request to Google's servers, which may log your IP address and browser information. This data is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Cookies and Tracking

This Site uses Google Analytics (GA4), a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to help us understand how visitors use the Site, such as pages viewed and approximate location derived from IP address. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information. We do not use tracking pixels, advertising networks, or any other behavioral tracking technology beyond Google Analytics. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Third-party services used for hosting, font delivery, and analytics (GitHub Pages, Google Fonts, and Google Analytics) may set their own cookies or employ their own tracking mechanisms. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those services directly, including Google's Privacy Policy.

3. How Information Is Used

We do not collect or process personal data. Any data collected by third-party infrastructure providers (GitHub, Google) is used by those providers in accordance with their own privacy policies and is not accessible to or controlled by us.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer any information about visitors to third parties. We have no data to share. If we were ever compelled by law to disclose user information, we would have nothing to provide — because we do not collect it.

5. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. External Links

The Site may contain links to external websites. This Privacy Policy applies only to illaskforit.com. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of any external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit. Some links on this Site are affiliate links, including links through the Amazon Associates Program. If you click one of these links and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details.

7. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to secure. The Site is served over HTTPS to protect data in transit between your browser and our hosting provider. No sensitive information is transmitted through this Site.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding personal data under laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), or other applicable privacy laws. Because we do not collect personal data, there is generally no data to access, correct, delete, or port. If you have questions or concerns about your rights, you may contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact

This is a personal opinion site. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise any rights, you can reach us through the contact information listed on the About page.
