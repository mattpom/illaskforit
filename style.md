# I'll Ask For It — Style Guide

Domain: illaskforit.com. Repo: mattpom/illaskforit.

## Identity

A personal, opinionated commentary site. Indianapolis is home base and a permanent nav category, but the brand voice is national in scope: one person's take on food, music, sports, travel, and nostalgia, not a local news outlet.

## Voice and Tone

Direct, dry, confident, a little combative in a fun way. States opinions as though they're settled fact, then winks at the reader about it. Every article headline carries the site name as a verbal tic, in the form "I'll Ask For It: ...".

## Color Palette

Background f5f5f5, card surfaces ffffff, dark surfaces 111111 and 222222. Accent crimson cc0000, darker accent aa0000. Borders use a translucent red, rgba(204,0,0,0.2).

## Typography

Headlines use Barlow Condensed, bold, uppercase, tight letter-spacing. Body text uses Barlow.

## Navigation

One Man's Opinion, Indy Institutions, Food and Drink, Music, Sports, Worth the Trip, Nostalgia, Shopping, Deeper Dive, About. Indy Institutions is one tab among many, not the homepage identity.

## Layout Patterns

A sticky top banner carries a timely, punchy one-liner in the brand's crimson accent (currently a football and Indiana line), matching the site's accent color rather than a caution-yellow construction look. A secondary scrolling ticker sits below the nav for category highlights. The homepage uses a card-grid structure: category tiles, Top 10 lists, and featured reads. A recurring pull-quote block reinforces the site's tagline.

## Deeper Dive

Internal page at /deeper-dive.html, embeds the shared Pinterest profile widget. Present in nav on every page with a real nav; listed in sitemap.xml.
