Top 10 Restaurants

The places that earned permanent real estate in your rotation. Not just good — consequential.

Words by Sean Matthews · Food & Drink · Top 10 List

← Back to Home

Not a Michelin guide. Not a Yelp dump. This is the list of places that changed how you think about eating out. Some are fancy. Some are holes in the wall. All of them are right. This is my rotation, not a democracy.

10
Pizzeria Bianco — Phoenix
Chris Bianco proved that one person's obsession can become the best pizza in America. The wait is real. The pizza is realer. If you've been, you know. If you haven't, you have a trip to plan.
Worth the flight
09
St. Elmo Steak House — Indianapolis
The shrimp cocktail will make your eyes water. The steaks are enormous. The history is longer than most careers. This is the restaurant Indianapolis uses to prove it has a restaurant.
Indy Institution
08
Slippery Noodle Inn — Indianapolis
The oldest bar in Indiana. Blues seven nights a week. You can feel the decades in the floor. Some places have history. This one is history.
Indy Original
07
Cafe Boulud — NYC
Daniel Boulud's prix-fixe changed the standard for what a meal could be. Technique you can taste. Service that doesn't make you feel watched. The kind of dinner you talk about for years.
Special Occasion
06
Café du Monde — New Orleans
Beignets and café au lait at 2 AM. Powdered sugar on your shirt. The sound of the Quarter carrying across the river. Some experiences are not food — they're time capsules.
Bucket List
05
Harry & Izzy's — Indianapolis
The steakhouse cousin that does everything right. The shrimp cocktail is famous for a reason. The pork chops are an argument-ender. Local legend that deserves national recognition.
Indy Best
04
Bar Nestor — San Sebastián
Pintxos at a counter you'll barely find. Txakoli poured from height. The anchor bar of the best eating neighborhood in the world. If you're in the Parte Vieja, you start here.
Worth the Trip
03
Portillo's — Chicago
The Italian beef is a religion. The cheese fries are a sacrament. Every city should have a Portillo's. Most cities don't deserve one. Chicago earned it.
Road Trip
02
Teibel's — Schererville, IN
A regional institution that serves fried chicken and perch in a way that makes you wonder why everything else bothers. Unchanged for decades. Unapologetic about it. Correct.
Hidden Gem
01
Bluebeard — Indianapolis
The restaurant that proved Mass Ave was ready. Farm-to-table that doesn't lecture you about it. Creative, consistent, and the kind of menu that makes you think about what's possible in this city.
Indy Modern
