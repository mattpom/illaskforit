Worth the Trip · Top 10 List

Top 10 Trails

The trails worth lacing up for. Some are in your backyard. Some require a road trip. All pay off.

Words by Sean Matthews · Worth the Trip · Top 10 List

← Back to Home

A trail is a promise. You put in the work at the beginning, and something waits at the end — a view, a waterfall, a moment of quiet you couldn't find anywhere else. These are the ten that kept their promise. Ranked in the order I'd actually go back.

10
Appalachian Trail — Springer Mtn to Katahdin
2,190 miles of forest, mountain, and whatever you're made of. Most people do a section. The through-hikers who finish it are a different category of human. Even a week on it will rearrange how you think about distance.
Life Changer
09
Angels Landing — Zion National Park, UT
The chain section. The exposure. The drop on either side that makes you question every decision that brought you here. And then the summit. One of the most dramatic views in the American West, earned.
Earn the View
08
Half Dome — Yosemite National Park, CA
The cables on the final pitch are steep enough to make your hands shake. The summit is worth every nerve. The valley view from the top justifies every permit application you ever filed.
Classic
07
Rim Trail — Grand Canyon South Rim, AZ
You can walk the entire South Rim on a paved trail and look into something that still doesn't make complete sense even when you're standing at the edge. Accessible. Overwhelming. Required.
Must Walk
06
Bluff Trail — Lookout Mountain, GA/TN
The ridge walk from Sunset Rock to Point Park, where Civil War soldiers fought above the clouds. History, cliff-edge views, and a straight shot down into a city that's done everything right with its waterfront.
Hidden
05
Brown County State Park Trails — Nashville, IN
Indiana's answer to the Smokies. Fall color that makes people drive three hours for it. The trails through the hills and hollers are what everyone who moved here from somewhere else didn't expect to find.
Indiana Best
04
Starved Rock State Park — Utica, IL
Eighteen canyons carved by glacial meltwater, two hours from Chicago. The canyon walls are sandstone. The waterfalls freeze solid in winter. It looks like something that shouldn't exist in Illinois.
Midwest Surprise
03
Tecumseh Trail — Morgan-Monroe State Forest, IN
The longest marked hiking trail in Indiana. Rugged for the Midwest. Quiet. The kind of trail that makes you realize Indiana has more vertical relief than people give it credit for.
Long Haul
02
Cliff Trail — Turkey Run State Park, IN
Rocky hollows, suspension bridges, ladders bolted into sandstone canyon walls. This is not a nature walk — it's an adventure. The best trails in Indiana are all in Turkey Run, and this is the best of those.
Indiana Adventure
01
Monon Trail — Indianapolis, IN
26 miles through the heart of Indianapolis, from Broad Ripple to downtown and beyond. The city's most democratic space. Runners, cyclists, dog walkers, strollers. The trail that proved Indianapolis could have nice things.
Daily Ritual
